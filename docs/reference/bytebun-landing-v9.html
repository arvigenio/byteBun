Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

Are the website, domain, and hosting really free?

Yes. The website, domain registration, hosting, and SSL certificate are included at no extra charge while you have an active plan. Plans have a minimum term, which we explain in your proposal before you sign.

What happens to my domain if I cancel?

After the minimum term is complete and your account is settled, we transfer the domain to you. The details are written into your agreement.

How fast can we go live?

Most Starter and Growth setups go live in two to four weeks, depending on how quickly you send your logo, services, and FAQs.

Will the AI chatbot say something wrong?

It only answers from information you approve. When it is unsure, it hands the conversation to your team instead of guessing, and it never gives medical advice.

Do I need to change my booking software?

Usually not. We can connect to common calendars and tools, or provide a booking calendar as part of your plan.

Can I cancel?

After the minimum term, yes, with 30 days notice. Your data stays yours.
